Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version: LoonieBooks never sends your ledger to us. There's nowhere for it to go.

  • No LoonieBooks accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no LoonieBooks server holding anyone's finances.
  • Your ledger stays in your browser, on your Windows PC or on a server you run yourself.
  • To show prices and exchange rates, the app downloads public data from a few other sites. Those requests are the same for everyone, and they don't include your accounts, amounts or transactions. The exceptions are listed below, and each one happens only when you choose it.

Where your ledger is kept

  • In the browser (demo.looniebooks.ca): in a private database inside that browser, on that device. The site only sends the app's files; it never receives your ledger.
  • In the Windows app: in a file on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\MapleLedger\ledger.db). If you turn on sharing, the other devices on your home network can open it after entering your household passcode. That traffic stays on your network.
  • On your own server: in that server's database. If you use a LoonieBooks server that someone else runs, that person holds your ledger, not the LoonieBooks project.

Statements, pasted text, screenshots and receipt photos you import are read on your own device, including the text recognition. Only the transactions and receipts you keep are saved, and only the last four digits of an account number.

What the app fetches from other sites

These requests go straight from your browser (or from your own LoonieBooks app or server) to the site named. Like any request on the internet, the site can see your IP address and the browser's usual request details. Each site follows its own privacy policy.

Exchange rates · Bank of Canada (bankofcanada.ca)

When
You have an account or holding in a currency other than Canadian dollars.
Sends
The currency code, such as USD. Nothing else.

Stock and fund prices · stocktreemap.com

When
You have investment holdings with symbols.
Sends
Nothing about your ledger. The app downloads the same price files for everyone and finds your holdings in them on your device.

Asking for a missing listing · stocktreemap.com

When
Only when you press the button to ask for symbols the price feed doesn't carry yet.
Sends
Those few symbols and their currency. No quantities, values, accounts or other holdings.

House price index · ownquity.com

When
You have a property with a city or area.
Sends
Nothing about your ledger. The whole index for Canada is downloaded and your property's area is looked up on your device, so where you live is never sent.

Option prices · Yahoo Finance

When
Only in the Windows app or on your own server, and only if you turn option prices on.
Sends
The option contract symbols you hold. Your own LoonieBooks asks Yahoo on your browser's behalf.

Sign-in · Google

When
Only on a self-hosted server, when you choose to sign in.
Sends
The usual Google sign-in. The server learns your Google account's ID, name and email.

The Windows app sends nothing else: no usage data, no crash reports, and it doesn't check for updates on its own. If you run your own server, you can point any of these feeds somewhere else or turn them off in the client's appsettings.json (see the README).

Cookies and browser storage

  • This website and the browser demo set no cookies and use no analytics.
  • The browser version keeps your ledger and a few preferences (such as the dashboard's chosen accounts) in the browser's own storage on your device. Clearing the site's data deletes them, so keep a backup.
  • The Windows app sets a cookie only on your household's other devices, to remember that they entered the passcode (until 30 days after it's last used). A self-hosted server sets a sign-in cookie when you sign in.

This website

looniebooks.ca is hosted by Cloudflare and demo.looniebooks.ca by GitHub Pages. Downloads and the source code are on GitHub. Like any web host, they may keep standard request logs (IP address, page and browser) under their own privacy policies. We don't receive anything from those logs that includes your ledger, because your ledger never reaches them.
